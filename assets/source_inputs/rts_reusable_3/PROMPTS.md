Generated with the built-in ChatGPT Images tool. Original transparent PNGs are kept in this folder. The game uses the separate HD and Pixel cutouts prepared by `tools/python/make_rts_reusable_3.py`.

The input images were the owner's headquarters sheet and desert art target, plus the Forward+ Carbon captures of the opening, northeast oasis and crystal plaza named in the experiment log. The bottom-right building was the headquarters reference. Uniforms were repainted on Carbon's eight-direction sprite sheets.
